## Why Care?

The context-vigilance kit has a baseline frontmatter template: `title`,
`date_created`, `date_modified`, `site_uuid`, `hex_code`, `authors`. Every
doc that carries all of them can be sorted, linked, and rolled up the same
way as every other doc in the tree.

This pass fills only what's missing, and only from facts. Nothing is
guessed, no existing value changes, and no date is set to the day of the
sweep.

## What's New?

- **`date_modified`** on nine docs, each the date of the file's last
  commit before this sweep (2026-08-18 for eight, 2026-10-06 for
  `Lossless-Citation-Standards.md`).
- **`date_created: 2026-05-01`** on the three blueprints that lacked it,
  taken from their `date_authored_initial_draft`.
- **`authors: [Michael Staton]`** on `workflow/2026-05-01.md`, its only
  human commit author.
- **`augmented_with` as a one-item list** on the three blueprints above
  and the Obsidian-API reminder; the tool strings are unchanged.

The other seven docs already carried the template and are untouched.
`workflow/2026-05-01.md` still has no `title`: its first H1 is the first
of seven worklist sections, not a title for the doc.

Part of the tree-wide sweep planned in the context-vigilance kit:
`ai-labs/context-vigilance-kit/context-v/plans/Retrofit-Every-Repo-to-OKF-Then-the-Kit-Frontmatter.md`.
